# House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption
Source: Section 10(13A) and Rule 2A, Income-tax Rules (incometax.gov.in). Available only in the old regime.

## How HRA exemption is calculated
If your salary includes HRA and you pay rent, the exempt portion is the lowest of three amounts: the actual HRA received; rent paid minus 10% of salary; and 50% of salary for Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai (40% for other cities). Here salary means basic pay plus dearness allowance.

## Documents for claiming HRA
Keep rent receipts and a rent agreement. If annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000, you must give your employer the landlord's PAN. Rent paid to parents is allowed if they actually own the house and declare the rent as income; rent paid to a spouse is generally not accepted.

## HRA and the new regime
HRA exemption is not available in the new regime. People paying high rent in a metro with a large HRA component are the most likely to benefit from the old regime, so compare both before choosing.
