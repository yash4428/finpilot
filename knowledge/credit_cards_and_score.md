# Credit cards and your credit score
Source: RBI Master Direction on Credit Card and Debit Card (2022) and credit bureau guidance from TransUnion CIBIL.

## How credit card interest works
If you pay the full statement balance by the due date, purchases are interest-free for the grace period, typically 20 to 50 days. If you pay less than the full amount, interest of roughly 3% to 3.75% a month (about 36% to 45% a year) is charged on the outstanding balance, and new purchases lose their interest-free period until the balance is cleared.

## Minimum amount due
The minimum amount due is usually around 5% of the outstanding plus any EMIs and fees. Paying only the minimum avoids a late fee but not interest. RBI rules require that a payment counts as late, and is reported to bureaus, only if it is more than 3 days past the due date.

## Credit utilisation
Credit utilisation is the share of your total card limit you are using. Keeping it under 30% is widely recommended for a healthy score. Utilisation is measured on the balance reported by the issuer, usually at the statement date, so paying down before the statement date lowers it.

## What a credit score is
In India, bureau scores such as CIBIL range from 300 to 900. A score of 750 or higher is generally seen as good and helps you get loans at lower interest rates. The biggest factors are repayment history, utilisation, length of credit history, credit mix and recent hard enquiries.

## How to improve a credit score
Pay every EMI and card bill on time; set up autopay for at least the minimum due. Keep utilisation below 30%. Avoid applying for many loans or cards in a short period. Keep old cards open to preserve history length. Check your credit report once a year for errors and dispute them with the bureau.
