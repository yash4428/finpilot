# Personal finance glossary
Source: FinPilot glossary, compiled from RBI, SEBI and Income Tax Department public material.

## EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment. A fixed monthly payment that repays a loan's principal and interest over its tenure. Early EMIs are mostly interest; later ones are mostly principal.

## APR and monthly interest
APR is the annual percentage rate on a borrowing. Credit cards often quote a monthly rate; multiplying by twelve gives a rough annual figure, and compounding makes the true cost higher.

## SIP
A Systematic Investment Plan invests a fixed amount in a mutual fund at a regular interval, usually monthly. It averages the purchase price over time and builds the habit of investing.

## ELSS
Equity Linked Savings Scheme. A diversified equity mutual fund that qualifies for section 80C, with a 3-year lock-in for each instalment.

## PPF
Public Provident Fund. A government-backed savings scheme with a 15-year tenure, tax-free interest and 80C eligibility, with an annual deposit limit of ₹1,50,000.

## NPS
National Pension System. A market-linked retirement scheme regulated by PFRDA. Contributions qualify under 80C and an extra ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B) in the old regime.

## TDS
Tax Deducted at Source. Tax your employer or bank deducts before paying you; it shows in Form 16 and Form 26AS and is adjusted against your final tax.

## AIS and Form 26AS
The Annual Information Statement and Form 26AS on the e-filing portal list income, TDS and high-value transactions reported against your PAN. Check them before filing.

## Safe to spend
FinPilot's measure of money that is genuinely free: your bank balance minus bills and EMIs already committed this cycle, and the balance you are predicted to have at month end after recurring and typical spending.
